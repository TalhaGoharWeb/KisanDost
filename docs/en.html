My Lands

🌱

My Crops

📝

Today's Work

A daily diary — watering, fertilizer, spray, labour, every job dated.

Expenses

Every rupee by category — pick a warehouse item and the cost fills in automatically.

🌾

Harvest & Sales

How much came in, sold to whom at what rate — receipts included.

How to use

Get started in seven easy steps

From first launch to seeing your profit — in order.

1

Download and install

Get the APK below, install it, and open the app.

2

Allow permissions

On first launch, allow notifications and alarms — your reminders depend on them.

3

Tour or try the demo

See the 3-page intro, or tap “View demo” to explore every screen with sample data.

4

Add your land and fields

In “My Lands”, create a farm and its fields — names and sizes.

5

Start a crop season

In “My Crops”, start a season — which crop, which field, sown when.

6

Record daily work and expenses

Log each day's work; when recording an expense, pick the warehouse item and cost and stock take care of themselves.

7

See harvest, sales and profit

Record the harvest, log sales, and open “Profit & Loss” for each crop's true account.
